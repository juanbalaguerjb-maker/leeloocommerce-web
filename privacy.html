Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

LeeLoo Commerce LLC ("LeeLoo", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through our website and business dealings, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Business contact details you provide (name, company or agency, email, phone, shipping and billing address).
  • Order and invoice information.
  • Payment information, which is collected and processed directly by our payment processor, Stripe. We do not receive or store full card numbers.
  • Basic technical data our hosting provider records automatically (such as IP address and browser type) for security and operation of the website.

How we use information

We use this information only to respond to inquiries, prepare quotes, process and deliver orders, issue invoices and refunds, comply with legal and tax obligations, and prevent fraud.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with service providers needed to run our business (payment processing, shipping carriers, banking, email and hosting), with government authorities when required by law, or with your consent.

Cookies

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our payment processor may use cookies necessary to process payments securely and prevent fraud.

Data retention and security

We keep business records for as long as required for legal, tax and accounting purposes, and protect them with reasonable administrative and technical safeguards.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, subject to our legal record-keeping obligations, by emailing info@leeloocommerce.com.

Changes
